Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-19

This application is a personal financial management tool used by the system owner for budgeting and financial tracking. It can connect to bank accounts through Enable Banking to retrieve account information and transaction data.

Data We Access

With explicit consent, the application may access:

  • Account balances
  • Transaction history
  • Account metadata, such as account name, account identifier, and currency
  • Other banking information made available by the connected financial institution through Enable Banking

How Data Is Used

Financial data is used solely for personal financial management, budgeting, and transaction tracking.

The data is not used for advertising, profiling, or resale by the system owner.

Data Sharing

Banking data may be processed by Enable Banking as necessary to provide bank-account connectivity.

The system owner does not intentionally sell or share personal financial data with unrelated third parties.

Data Storage

Financial data may be stored on the self-hosted server running this application.

The system owner is responsible for the security and protection of data stored on that server. Data may also be processed by third-party services involved in providing bank connectivity or other functionality enabled by the application.

Third-Party Services

When connecting a bank account, Enable Banking and the relevant financial institution may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

Contact

This application is maintained for personal use. For questions concerning this application or its operation, contact the system owner directly.
